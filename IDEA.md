**agent-hub** — A central registry and orchestration platform for discovering, deploying, and managing AI agents.

- Provide a searchable public registry where developers can publish agents with versioned metadata, capabilities, and pricing.
- Enable one-click deployment of agents to cloud, local, or edge environments with isolated sandboxes.
- Define a lightweight inter-agent communication protocol so agents can delegate tasks and share context.
- Offer a unified dashboard for monitoring agent health, usage metrics, logs, and cost tracking.
- Support a plugin/skill marketplace so agents can dynamically extend their capabilities at runtime.
