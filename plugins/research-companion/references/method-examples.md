# Optional method and presentation examples

These examples illustrate past or domain-specific choices. They are not default
requirements. Select them only when they fit the scientist's question, inputs,
and requested outputs, and record the selection in that analysis plan.

## Descriptive current-voltage sweep

A plateau-current extraction, an effective-resistance curve, or a selected
voltage-sweep overlay may be useful when the question and supplied data call for
it. State the extraction window, sample unit, missing-data handling and whether
the result is descriptive. A descriptive fit can summarize the selected points;
it does not establish an inferential relationship by itself.

## Figure style and presets

A Paper USTH preset, one-color line, connected markers, or shaded time window may
be chosen for a particular publication or figure. These are examples of local
style choices. The planner and implementation owner must use the selected
project's declared palette/preset and the user's requirements rather than
applying one as a global rule.
