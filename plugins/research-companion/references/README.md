# Shared Research References & Contracts

This directory provides the authoritative contracts, definitions, and handoff protocols shared across all skills in the `research-companion` plugin:
- `consultation.md`: Guidelines for `ask-einstein` grounded research consultation.
- `plans.md`: HTML research plan schemas, authoring guidelines, and review states for `plan-research`.
- `study-planning.md`: Study-level method decomposition and parameter boundaries.
- `transitions.md`: Shared owner, write-permission and handoff contract across Study, plan-led analysis and Research Progress.
- `method-examples.md`: Optional case-specific analysis and styling examples; these are not defaults.
- `progress-synthesis.md`: Research Progress-owned evidence ingestion and presentation actions.

## Fundamental Concepts

- **Project**: The overarching research purpose, objectives, and active physical devices.
- **Study**: One bounded empirical or computational question with explicit inputs and reproducible run evidence.
- **Plan**: A proposed, reviewed, or approved research roadmap (`plans/`).
- **Observation**: The factual measurement derived directly from instrument raw data.
- **Interpretation**: Scientific inference or hypotheses that could explain the observation.
- **Artifact disposition**: Explicit decision about selecting an output (ACCEPTED, REJECTED, AMBIGUOUS, SUPERSEDED, MISSING).
- **Scientific acceptance**: Separate scientist decision; remains `NOT_ASSESSED` unless explicitly assessed.
