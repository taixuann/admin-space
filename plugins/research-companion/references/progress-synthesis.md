# Progress Synthesis & Presentation Delivery

This reference governs how empirical Study evidence flows upward into project-level Goals and Presentation Decks.

---

## 1. Directory Structure of `progress/`

The `progress/` workspace consolidates all persistent documentation under `docs/` and all presentation deliverables under `presentation/`:

```text
progress/
├── docs/
│   ├── goals/
│   │   ├── G-switching-dynamics.md       # Long-form, comprehensive goal documents
│   │   └── G-drift-analysis.md
│   ├── plans/
│   │   ├── 2026-10-20_weekly_plan.html   # Presentation & reporting plan
│   │   └── 2026-10-27_monthly_plan.html
│   └── log.md                            # Central provenance ledger for ingested runs & goals
└── presentation/
    └── weekly/
        └── 2026-10-20/                   # Dedicated, self-contained dated run folder
            ├── weekly_report.html        # HTML presentation slides
            ├── weekly_report.pptx        # PowerPoint presentation
            ├── weekly_report.pdf         # Vector PDF export
            └── assets/                   # Slide figures, logos, styling assets
```

## 2. Research Progress owns evidence ingestion into Goals and `log.md`

A Goal in `progress/docs/goals/G-*.md` connects research objectives with concrete Study runs:

1. **Locate Sealed Study Locks & Results**:
   - Check `studies/<study-slug>/runs/<run-id>/run.lock.yaml` and `studies/<study-slug>/results/RESULT.md`.
   - Never cite unverified, in-progress, or failed runs.
2. **When explicitly requested, Research Progress records the selected synthesis in `progress/docs/log.md`**:
   - The Research Progress owner appends a provenance-linked entry such as:
     ```markdown
     ## [2026-10-20] Study Ingestion: <study-slug> / <run-id>
     - **Goal Ref**: [[G-switching-dynamics.md]]
     - **Artifact disposition**: ACCEPTED (not scientific acceptance)
     - **Primary Figures**: `figures/primary/<bundle>/<fig-id>.pdf` (SHA-256: `<hash>`)
     - **Key Finding**: <One-line factual summary of physical/empirical observation>
     ```
3. **Record Evidence Bindings in Goal**:
   - Record exact paths and SHA-256 hashes of primary figure bundles:
     `studies/<study-slug>/runs/<run-id>/figures/primary/<bundle>/<fig-id>.pdf`
   - Preserve the source record's stated scientific acceptance separately from the artifact disposition.
4. **Goal Status States**:
   - `ACTIVE`: Current focus, with specific next questions.
   - `BLOCKED`: Awaiting external decision, device fabrication, or missing input.
   - `DONE`: Completed with verified evidence links and numbers (never subjective claims).
   - `DROPPED`: Deprecated with a one-line reason.

## 3. Presentation Lifecycle (`plans/` $\to$ `presentation/`)

Creating a weekly or milestone presentation follows the standard progression:

### Research Progress: Presentation Plan (`progress/docs/plans/`)
- Author standalone HTML presentation plan (`progress/docs/plans/<date>_weekly_plan.html`).
- Defines:
  1. Target audience and purpose.
  2. Slide storyboard (title, key takeaway, required figures per slide).
  3. Evidence bindings: explicit links to sealed `run.lock.yaml` and `log.md` entries.
  4. Template and design constraints (following `consulting-pptx` rules).

### Deck construction (`consulting-pptx`)
- Research Progress owns the requested plan and coordinates slide construction via `consulting-pptx`.
- Each execution creates a self-contained dated folder: `progress/presentation/weekly/<date>/`.
- Preserves all deliverables generated by `consulting-pptx`:
  - Primary HTML interactive deck: `<purpose>.html`
  - PowerPoint presentation: `<purpose>.pptx`
  - Vector PDF slide export: `<purpose>.pdf`
  - All embedded figure copies and supporting metadata.
- **Fail-closed**: Any slide referencing unsealed evidence or broken figure paths aborts the render.
